\section*{Data and Software Availability}
The five Pfam seed alignments are publicly available from InterPro under the accession numbers
listed in Materials and Methods. The curated SwissProt $\omega$-conotoxin alignment,
designation audit, canonical per-replicate data, generated tables, matched profile-HMM
emissions, and ESM2 scoring outputs are included in the study repository at
\url{https://github.com/varnerlab/SA-Binding-Generation-Study}. The repository also provides
the Julia and Python scripts needed to reproduce the benchmark calculations and all reported
display items. No proprietary data were used.
