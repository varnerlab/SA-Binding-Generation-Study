% abstract.tex -- Abstract
Small protein-family alignments may contain a subset of interest but too little labeled data
for training a conditional generator. We address this regime by adding one multiplicity ratio
to the logits of a training-free stochastic-attention sampler, shifting generation from the
full family toward designated sequences. For unit-norm memories, the Boltzmann target is an
exact Gaussian mixture whose component weights are the normalized multiplicities. This result
separates exact latent conditioning from losses introduced by finite-temperature variation,
PCA reconstruction, and discrete decoding. Across five Pfam families, attention tracked the
analytic target, while decoded single-position markers depended on subset separation. A matched
weighted profile HMM transferred these markers more directly, whereas stochastic attention had
lower ESM2 pseudo-perplexity in the matched Kunitz comparison. Curated seeding of 23
$\omega$-conotoxin accessions produced diverse sequences that retained the cysteine scaffold,
Tyr13, and several reported residue frequencies. These sequences are testable candidates, not
evidence of binding.
